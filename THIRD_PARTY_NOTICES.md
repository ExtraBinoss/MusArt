The local TSL implementation references the owner's [ts-slug](https://github.com/ExtraBinoss/ts-slug); its provenance and algorithm credits are recorded in [the text-rendering notes](docs/rendering/analytical-text.md). The ignored reference clone is not redistributed or imported.

Twenty outline font families come from Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License. Each family retains its OFL notice in `apps/web/public/fonts/`; the [font manifest](apps/web/public/fonts/manifest.json) records exact sources and checksums. Magic Search uses [cmdk](https://github.com/pacocoursey/cmdk) 1.1.1 (MIT) through the official shadcn Command component.
